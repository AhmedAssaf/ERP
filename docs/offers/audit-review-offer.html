Procurement audit review
for private companies in Saudi Arabia

We check 100% of your purchasing, not a sample

Internal audit usually tests a small sample of purchase orders. Nobody sees the rest: orders split to stay under an approval limit, one vendor taking everything, invoices paid twice. We check every purchase order and invoice in a quarter with eight ready-made tests, and hand you an audit file you can take to the audit committee.

Eight ready-made checks

1Split orders: several POs to one vendor, each just under an approval limit
2Single source: one vendor takes most of a category's spend
3Duplicate invoices: the same invoice or amount paid twice
4Conflict of interest: a vendor's bank account matches a staff account
5New vendor: paid within days of being created
6Price outliers: unit price far above the usual for the same item
7Weak approvals: requester approved own PO, or approved at night or on the weekend
8Invoice patterns: round amounts or invoice numbers in a row

What you receive

  • An audit file (PDF) with findings ranked by amount and the evidence for each
  • The findings in Excel for your internal audit team
  • A 30-minute walk-through with the CFO or head of audit
  • Practical recommendations: what to tighten, and when to run a competitive tender

Your data is protected

  • Non-disclosure agreement and written consent before we receive any file
  • Processed in Saudi Arabia only, deleted after 30 days
  • Staff bank accounts are compared in encrypted form and never appear in any report
