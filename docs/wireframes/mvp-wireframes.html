17 screens: the buying company's workspace, then the vendor portal. Colours and type come from the design system (document 08).

Low-fidelity: grey blocks stand for content, while buttons and states are real. Every label is written in full, with no abbreviations. Switch the language to see the right-to-left layout. Vendor screens are phone width because vendors mostly submit from a phone.

Blue: continue, submit, or approve Red: close, cancel, or reject White: neutral action such as save or go back Link: open or download

Component key (document 08, section 6): App header, buttons, form fields, step indicator, stage timeline, sealed envelope, status badges, confirmation dialogs, history list, data table. Purple is kept only for sealed and locked states.

Button colours: Blue is for continuing, submitting, or approving. Red is for closing, cancelling, or rejecting. White is for neutral actions. These colours are fixed and do not change with the company's brand colour.

Deliberately left out: custom web address, committees, tender amendments, vendor dashboard, text messages, artificial intelligence. All come after the pilot (document 05, section 4).
